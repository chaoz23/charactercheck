# Third-party notices

## MrPrimate/ddb-importer

CharacterCheck's initial offline D&D Beyond adapter registry was independently
observed from D&D Beyond configuration data and cross-checked against the
`data/fallback-config.json` artifact at ddb-importer commit
`0227f9cb5bee6ec74ac6ea2956b22da4cd7b4460`. Narrow ID/name mapping facts and
the snapshot/fallback design were adapted with reference to that MIT-licensed
project. The distributed artifact is a purpose-limited allowlist, not the full
DDB or ddb-importer configuration. CharacterCheck does not make ddb-importer
or Foundry a runtime dependency.
